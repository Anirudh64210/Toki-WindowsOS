# 18 · A fresh coat of paint, and notes that fit the meeting

A big polish pass, in plain terms.

**Light mode.** There's now a little sun/moon button in the top corner of Settings. Tap it and the whole app - the notch and your Library - switches between the dark look and a clean white one, like flipping a light switch. No restart. The white is a soft, warm white, not a harsh "x-ray" white.

**The notch is see-through now.** Like frosted glass or a tinted mirror - you can faintly make out the colours of whatever's behind it. It's kept mostly solid so the text stays easy to read.

**Notes that fit the meeting.** Toki now notices what *kind* of meeting it was and shapes the notes to match. A daily standup gets "Updates" and "Blockers." An interview gets "Strengths," "Concerns," and a recommendation. A decision meeting lists the options and what was decided. And when someone is handed a task, Toki tags it with their name - but only if it actually heard the name, never a guess.

**Toki looks like Toki.** The little character is now the pink cloud from the original design (with its three puffy humps), not a plain blob.

**Smoother everywhere.** Opening and closing the notch, opening the Library, and scrolling all glide now instead of jumping.

**Tidier settings.** The light/dark button and the "show while screen-recording" button now sit once, up in the corner, instead of cluttering every page. The recording button is a small camera that's crossed out when Toki is hidden - which is its normal, private state.
