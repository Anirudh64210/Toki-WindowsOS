# 19 · Toki gets a home

Opening your Library used to just show a list. Now it opens a proper **home** - a bit like the ChatGPT or Claude desktop apps.

Down the left is a **sidebar**: Toki (the pink cloud - give it a poke, it reacts), three places to go - **Ask**, **Library**, and **Templates** - and a **Back to Toki** button at the bottom.

**Ask** is the front page: a friendly greeting that types itself out (different each time), and a big search box in the middle. Right now, asking something takes you to your Library and searches it - but next (Phase 2) you'll be able to actually *ask Toki questions about your meetings* and get answers back, by typing or by voice.

**Library** is your meetings, same as before - but now there's a little **reorder switch** next to the search. It's off normally so your notes never get dragged around by accident; flip it on when you want to rearrange them. And when you open a meeting, there's a **Template** picker at the top - change it and Toki re-writes that note in the new shape.

**Templates** shows all the note shapes Toki can use, and what each one includes.

It's the *shell* of something bigger - the "ask your notes" brain is what comes next.
