# The story of building Toki for Windows

This folder explains the build in plain language - no jargon, no code. One short file per step: what we set out to do, what broke, and how it was fixed.

Read them in order:

1. [Getting started](01-getting-started.md) - setting up the workshop and the empty app.
2. [The pill is alive](02-the-pill.md) - Toki's body: the shape-shifting pill, the tray icon, and the shortcut.
3. [Toki can hear](03-toki-can-hear.md) - the two ears (your mic + the meeting app only), and proof it records only the right app.
4. [Toki notices, and a real app you can open](04-noticing-and-recording.md) - double-click app, never-crash, ghost mode, and detecting a call to ask about recording.
5. [Never freezes, and properly alive](05-smooth-and-alive.md) - killing the freeze bug, re-launch handling, and the redone alive loading animation.
6. [The black window, and why the PC got slow](06-black-window-fixed.md) - the see-through-window vs ghost-mode conflict, and rebuilding the window to be solid, fast, and stable.
7. [Toki turns talking into text](07-toki-understands-speech.md) - on-device speech-to-text, both sides transcribed and labelled, echo removed.
8. [Toki remembers, and pulls out the useful bits](08-remembering-meetings.md) - local database, first-pass notes, the searchable Library, and manual notes.
9. [Toki writes the notes like a person would](09-ai-written-notes.md) - the on-device AI writer, three-pass notes, safety net, and the GPU control.
10. [Making the notes better, cleaner, and faster](10-refined-and-faster.md) - Granola-style notes, concise titles, no ugly first draft, a big speed-up, the Library window, and the honest story on transcription speed.
11. [Making Toki fit your PC](11-fits-your-pc.md) - the lost-meeting bug explained, capturing the other people on a manual start, long meetings that can't crash, and fitting two big models into a small memory.
12. [Real glass, calm recording, cleaner buttons](12-glass-and-polish.md) - the calm recording chip, click-outside that stops closing things, a genuinely translucent pill that's still invisible to recording, crisp icons, and a design-matched Library.
13. [Honest transcripts and quiet recording](13-honest-notes-and-quiet-recording.md) - the mid-word transcript bug found and fixed (900+ fragments → 82 clean sentences), the truth about the "6 minutes", skipping a muted mic, the tiny live-Toki recording pill, and small UI fixes.
14. [Toki moves to the top, like a notch](14-the-notch.md) - the pivot to a top-edge virtual notch: Toki peeks first then opens your page, closes off cleanly so it never squats on your work (and the summon that finally "sticks" past the browser tabs), flips upside-down while recording, and the fixes along the way.
15. [Notes that are actually smart (and honest)](15-smarter-notes.md) - reading a long meeting in pieces so the summary covers the *whole* thing, notes shaped to the meeting (no made-up filler for a nothing-chat), and the slow-speech transcript bug fixed.
16. [Why notes take time (and how the fast apps cheat)](16-why-notes-take-time.md) - the honest speed story: cloud apps rent a 10× bigger engine, on-device keeps your meetings yours but slower, why "summarize during the meeting" hurts quality, and the two real fixes (bring-your-own-key for cloud speed, speculative decoding for the local model).
17. [Bring your own AI key](17-your-own-ai-key.md) - the optional cloud-speed path: pick a provider, paste a key, pick a model; how the key is kept safe; the honest "https is the same, the address isn't" fix; one green connected light; and the false starts (Claude-as-default, the clunky wheel, the erased key).
18. [A fresh coat of paint, and notes that fit the meeting](18-look-and-notes.md) - a live light/dark switch, a see-through "tinted mirror" notch, notes shaped to the kind of meeting (standup, interview, decision…) with name-tagged tasks, the pink-cloud mascot, smoother motion everywhere, and tidier settings.
19. [Toki gets a home](19-the-hub.md) - the Library becomes a Claude-desktop-style hub: a sidebar (Ask / Library / Templates), a typing greeting + search on the front page, a clickable mascot, an on/off reorder switch, and a per-note template picker. The shell before the chatbot.
20. [Notes you can copy, and a tidy Library](20-copyable-notes-and-tidy-library.md) - every part of a note becomes selectable and copyable (nothing locked as a picture), a one-click "copy everything," and a cleaner Library.
21. [Ask Toki - the chatbot](21-ask-toki-the-chatbot.md) - the assistant arrives: ask questions about your meetings and get streamed answers pulled from your own notes.
22. [Ask Toki gets smart](22-ask-toki-gets-smart.md) - the assistant learns to route questions (facts vs. thinking vs. commands), ground to the meeting on screen, know people's names, and stop making things up.
23. [Toki learns to talk](23-toki-learns-to-talk.md) - the dialogue brain: Toki remembers who you are, can just chat, keeps meeting answers grounded, and never fabricates.
24. [Toki connects to your calendar](24-connecting-your-calendar.md) - the private, safe link to Google Calendar (the foundation): browser-direct sign-in, an encrypted "valet key," the smallest possible permission, and proof it reads/creates events live.
25. [Your week, in the notch](25-calendar-in-the-notch.md) - the calendar UI: the redesigned home card ("COMING UP" + Join), an agenda-style week view with a "now" line, one-box natural-language create, edit, and delete (instant + Undo for solo events, confirm for guested ones).
26. [Toki preps you before a meeting](26-prep-cards.md) - the prep card: ~5 min before a meeting, Toki surfaces last time's recap, still-open items (with owners), and the agenda, plus Join-and-record. Deterministic (no model), honest when it's a first-time meeting, and restrained (once per meeting, only when idle).
27. [“Toki, schedule a call with Ana tomorrow at 3”](27-voice-create.md) - create a meeting by voice: Toki hears the request, reads back the title + time, and only books it after you say “yes” (a mishearing costs one “no”). Knows a request from a question, honest about names-vs-emails, and “at 3” now means 3 PM.
28. [A premium coat of paint (and a real glass notch)](28-premium-redesign-and-glass.md) - the redesign pass: a tight type scale, the redesigned green checkbox + toggle switch, colour-coded notes, the flattened composer, and a real DWM acrylic frosted-glass notch (theme-aware, one size, flush top).
29. [The hardening pass](29-the-hardening-pass.md) - nothing new to look at, everything sturdier: a full audit of the app, a live mic that can never be hidden, deleting a meeting deletes its audio, and surviving a crash mid-recording.
30. ["Tasks this week", a proper sort menu, and a lock on the front door](30-tasks-this-week-and-a-lock-on-the-door.md) - tasks answered by *when they're due* instead of when they were mentioned, a real sort menu in the Library, and a batch of quiet security fixes.
31. [A thousand questions](31-a-thousand-questions.md) - writing 1,155 test questions to find out where the assistant actually gets things wrong, then fixing what they exposed.
32. [A long day of real use](32-a-long-day-of-real-use.md) - the use-it-and-fix-what-hurts day: the scroll that fought back, text cut off everywhere, first-run onboarding and your name, a real conversation for making meetings, and the day voice "broke" (it was a muted mic).
33. [The premium pass](33-the-premium-pass.md) - settings get one home, the invisible-Toki bug (Windows kept throwing away "hide me from recordings"), "two meetings, five tasks each - why does it say five?", the calendar/prep/recording redesigns, and the app icon that refused to update.
34. [Notes you can actually write in](34-notes-you-can-write-in.md) - a real note editor (tick boxes, headings, lists - no fonts or colours, on purpose), the prep card you can write *on* instead of beside, Keep-or-Discard instead of saving behind your back, what you type steering the AI summary, and a ↳ on every point that jumps to the moment in the transcript it came from (and stays silent when it can't be traced).
35. [Sticky notes, done properly (and the bullet bug)](35-sticky-notes-done-properly.md) - why bullets were breeding on every save (and the test that pretends to be your fingers), taking the AI's hands off your summary, sticky notes as real records you *choose* rather than ones Toki guesses at by title, and the meeting-is-starting nudge coming back.
36. [The polish pass (and the bug that was eating your sentences)](36-the-polish-pass.md) - "mosquito sized text" turned out to be deleting what you typed, not just shrinking it; em dashes removed everywhere (including from what the AI writes, as it streams); one font weight across every page with Settings as the reference; and buttons whose box you can actually hit, because the hover state was invisible on transparent buttons.
37. [The first real meetings](37-the-first-real-meetings.md) - two actual recordings found what 1,800 tests could not: the Summary that was just raw transcript (and the title it poisoned), a transcript shredded into 182 fragments with people's sentences cut in half, "Greg" that was Craig, and a roll-call of eleven names that became eleven to-dos. Plus why Toki can't yet tell your colleagues apart, and how it's going to.
38. [The smoothness pass](38-the-smoothness-pass.md) - why the notch rendered "hard and ugly", and the motion rules that fixed it.
39. [Claude Design, and one Toki](39-claude-design-and-one-toki.md) - the design system mirrored to claude.ai/design, Ask and Settings rebuilt from the frames, and the four fixes from your own check.
40. [The five pill states](40-the-five-pill-states.md) - what the Figma export actually contained (23 screens, not a slice), the six menu-bar frames built to their exact numbers, the "Notes ready" pill that replaced a blank strip, the pause button I refused to draw because nothing can pause a capture, and the stale July binary that cost half an hour.
41. [Half your transcript was invented](41-half-your-transcript-was-invented.md) - why talat.app's transcripts beat ours on the same models: it was never the models. Your mic records 38dB quieter than the meeting audio, and a recogniser handed near-silence does not return nothing, it returns confident nonsense. A gate that can say "nobody spoke here", levelling that lifts the quiet stream and leaves the good one alone, the four mistakes I made getting there, and the one Windows setting worth more than all of it.
42. [Toki starts acting](42-toki-starts-acting.md) - the mascot revamp from your Grok bot reference, plus the addendum after "it only blinks": Toki now follows your pointer, darts its eyes on its own when nobody is near, never sits perfectly still, turns its sparkles, and writes in phrases instead of on a metronome. Also your Ask-page line, and the mascot revamp from your Grok bot reference: props that grow out of the body instead of appearing, a body that leans, dips in time with its own pen and squints when pleased, and eyes that look at whatever it is doing. Plus the mascot shadow that was re-rendering in software every frame, the notch's glass highlight that had been painted transparent since the redesign, and the test whose two comparison photographs had been byte-identical since the day it was written.
43. [The notes stopped lying](43-the-notes-stopped-lying.md) - what was wrong with your two Sunday recordings: the name fixer that turned "day" into "Toki" and "get" into "Craig" (and why the summary then repeated the invention), the silence gate that was guarding a road your meetings never drive on, the "Still open from last time" section handing you strangers' to-dos through word fragments and a ghost meeting from July, and the due date that was actually my own instruction leaking through the AI. All four fixed, all four now tests.
44. [Five critics walked the pipeline](44-five-critics-walked-the-pipeline.md) - you asked for agents to criticise the whole processing system; five did, 45 accusations came back, 24 were real, all fixed: the stopwatch that deleted whole meetings, the deleted meetings that resurrected, your ticks and titles being erased during the AI wait, the owner-check that graded the AI's own homework, one voice split into four people, Tim becoming Tom, and the echo maths that ran backwards.
45. [Toki learns to grade itself](45-toki-learns-to-grade-itself.md) - the accuracy report card (`--wertest`): read a two-minute script, get a word-error grade plus a per-NAME scorecard - and on its very first run it caught that pinning "Abhishek" could never have repaired "Ubishek". Plus the readability pass: "uh july twenty second" now saves as "July 22nd", fillers and stutters gone, but "two questions" stays words and "Mm-hmm" stays an answer - rules only, so it cannot invent.
46. [The waveform that actually hears you](46-the-waveform-that-hears-you.md) - there were two waveforms and neither one was listening: both danced the same whether you were talking, silent, or unplugged. Now there is one, used in both places, driven by the real microphone (and by the meeting's own audio, so it moves when the other person talks) - with silence drawn as resting dots. Plus the trick that makes your very quiet mic still fill the row, the ugly chevron replaced by a glass button that turns over, and the new check that caught two things I had reasoned my way past.
47. [The notch became glass](47-the-notch-became-glass.md) - the Apple Control Center look, built the way Apple actually builds it: why "real" blur is impossible for our window (and why that barely matters), the card as a layered material (gradient plate, invisible grain, a rim that catches the light at the top), glass-on-glass buttons that brighten on hover instead of greying, real shadows the pills had only ever been promised, the true Apple corner curve (and the honest measurement that you cannot see it at our size), and the edge fix that actually makes borders crisp on scaled displays.
48. [The details pass](48-the-details-pass.md) - your screenshot roll, itemised and fixed: the chevron button that was 26 pixels in a 24-pixel row, the play triangle that was a FONT character and could never centre, the lipstick red replaced by Apple's actual system red, the Library colour bars that were roulette now meaning blue = recorded and purple = manual, the corner glitch that was two Tokis and a one-frame vanish, the loader that now sits flush and actually tucks (its timer used to fire once and die under your cursor), and the peeking Toki with both eyes back and one unhurried half-second flip instead of two jittery bounces.
49. [The film round](49-the-film-round.md) - your screen recording solved it: the white flicker was the meeting prompt's white pill rendering ALONE for a few frames (the peek did not know how to bloom into it, and a background grow-step fired early), the loader WAS tucking and being silently un-tucked by the corner-curve settle asking the window instead of the card (while Toki stood outside the clipping altogether), the new rhythm - sliver, dwell to open, leave to tuck, 2-second "Notes ready" from Toki's bubble - the chevron's lean that was one hardware pixel of uneven margin, and the Y pinned to its ceiling by line spacing inherited from the paragraph around it.
50. [The ghost frame](50-the-ghost-frame.md) - why the corner flicker survived two rounds of fixes: it was WINDOWS replaying a stale snapshot of the half-closed card (these transparent windows keep their last drawn picture, and we said "hide me" one frame too early), which is also why both themes showed it; the notch now proves two real frames have drawn before hiding, so the snapshot is empty. Plus Toki's new flight: bubble dissolves, hop, one somersault, shrink, and a 560ms arc up to the peek spot - and the three filmed landing attempts it took to get there without resurrecting the pill.
