## What happened today, in one paragraph

The owner tested the build and found, over three rounds, roughly a dozen defects - and the pattern
across them matters more than any one fix: almost every defect was invisible to 1,900 green tests
because the tests measured mechanisms while the owner measured the product. The sticky note passed a
focus test and could not take a keystroke. The evidence clip passed a byte-accuracy test and played
the word "That". The pickers scrolled perfectly and offered 14 days of a calendar and 14 hours of a
day. The session's real output is not the fixes - it is the question now written into memory:
**would Outlook ship this?**

## What we learned (the expensive kind)

1. **"Would Outlook ship this?"** - the product question that catches what mechanism-testing cannot.
   Open the household-name reference BEFORE building any control that has one. (Memory:
   `would-outlook-ship-this`.)
2. **A clickable thing is a Button.** Third violation, third identical failure - a Border with a
   mouse handler inside a popup or a document silently never fires. The rule was already written in
   the codebase both times.
3. **A test that bypasses the real path passes on a broken product.** `DebugType` inserted text
   through the editor's API while real keystrokes died in the outer RichTextBox; the focus assertion
   passed while typing was impossible. The sticky test now asserts the STRUCTURE (no RichTextBox
   ancestor), because the structure was the bug.
4. **Self-tests must pin ambient settings.** The owner's first real use of the size dial broke 23
   assertions in a harness that inherited their saved preference.
5. **Debug state must be pinned against live refreshes.** `--measuretest`'s "4px clip" was its two
   passes measuring two different layouts, because showing the window let RefreshGoogle override
   DebugConnected.
6. **Grep for `error`, not `error CS`.** A XAML MC3000 failure hid behind that filter and two "fixes"
   were tested against a stale binary.
7. **Diagnostics that cry wolf are worse than none.** The click/hover logger reported three false
   failures (rectangle tests on rounded controls; only ButtonBase counted as clickable) while the
   real bug - a page closing itself on a timer - was unlogged.
8. **Research pays in constants.** The audio padding, the snap-into-silence rule, the 1.5s floor,
   the seek-vs-clip tradeoff, and the home hierarchy all came straight out of one research pass over
   Otter/Fathom/Fireflies/Descript/Granola/Fantastical.
